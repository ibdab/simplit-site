FAQ

Quick answers.

Does Simplit work in other Mac apps?+

Yes. Simplit is designed for Safari, Chrome, Notes, Mail, documents, worksheets, and other normal Mac workflows.

Where do the AI keys go?+

The API keys belong on your backend. The Mac app signs in and sends requests to your server, so users cannot open the app and steal your keys.

What is free vs Pro?+

Free is for core text actions with usage limits. Pro is for image tools, sneaky mode, faster premium responses, and higher limits.

How do I sign in or create an account?+

Download the Mac app and sign in or create an account from Simplit. The website is for downloads, pricing, and payment checkout.

Can people bypass the free limit?+

The proper setup limits usage from the backend by account and device identity. That makes simple account switching much harder to abuse.
